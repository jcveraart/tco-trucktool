Switching guide

Zero-emission zones: can your van or truck enter the city?

Check access by registration, municipality and travel date. A low-emission zone differs from a zero-emission zone. Vehicle category, emissions class, first registration and any exemption determine access.

Good to know

  • The rules are national, but each municipality decides whether and when to introduce a zone and how large it is.
  • Plug-in hybrid trucks can receive an exemption until 1 January 2030 if they demonstrably operate electrically within the zone.
  • Municipalities offer exemptions, such as day permits or exemptions for special-purpose vehicles.
  • Enter your current diesel registration in the calculator to see zone access deadlines and when switching could offer the greatest benefit.

Start with the municipality and registration check

Use the official registration check and verify boundaries and start dates with the relevant municipality. Distinguish current rules from announced changes in your replacement plan. A proposed extension does not confirm access.
